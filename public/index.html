01 / What I Do

Creative vision.
Meaningful stories.

From finding the heart of an idea to bringing it to the screen, I collaborate with creative teams at different stages of the storytelling process.

01

Story Consulting

Providing professional feedback on existing concepts, scripts, treatments, book drafts, and rough cuts, with actionable recommendations to strengthen narrative structure, character development, and emotional impact.

02

Story Producing

Developing and shaping narratives for documentaries and unscripted productions. From identifying the story’s through-line and planning interviews to reviewing footage and collaborating with editors, I guide the narrative from pre-production through post-production.

03

Creative Direction & Directing

Developing original concepts and directing cinematic stories for films, commercials, and campaigns. From creative treatments and visual direction to on-set execution, I shape how a story looks, feels, and comes to life.

04

Film & Video Producing

Providing hands-on producing support for narrative films, short films, commercials, and video productions. I collaborate with existing teams, bring together additional creative and production professionals as needed, and oversee production execution from planning through completion.

04 / The Artist

Meet Brittney.

Brittney Michelle is a filmmaker, screenwriter, director, and producer with a passion for telling stories that explore the complexities of the human experience.

Her work spans narrative film, documentary, and branded storytelling, united by a belief that the most compelling stories are the ones that make us feel something.

As the writer, director, and producer of the independent film Friendsgiving, Brittney brings her storytelling perspective to original film and television projects while collaborating with brands, organizations, and creative teams to develop stories that connect with audiences.

With a background in creative leadership and multimedia production, she combines an artist's perspective with the experience to bring ambitious ideas from concept to screen.
